# Figure 4: Intercellular Communication Networks (Hippocampus)

> **Plain-Language Summary for Collaborators**  
> In the Hippocampus, memory circuits rely entirely on tight communication between neurons, supporting glia, and microvascular capillaries.  
> In Step 4, we use **CellChat v2.2** to eavesdrop on the cellular telephone calls between hippocampal cell types to discover how disease disrupts intercellular communication in the brain's memory hub.

---

## 🖼️ Panel Walkthrough (What You Are Looking At)

### 1. `Fig4a_intercellular_interaction_strength.*` — Total Telephone Call Volume
- **What it shows**: Comparison of total interaction counts and interaction strength between Control and Alzheimer's in the Hippocampus.

### 2. `Fig4b_differential_interaction_network.*` — Broken vs. Jammed Phone Lines
- **What it shows**: Circular diagram showing net shifts in hippocampal cellular crosstalk:
  - **Red lines**: Channels that became abnormally hyper-activated in Alzheimer's.
  - **Blue lines**: Healthy communication channels that were lost or silenced.

### 3. `Fig4c_signaling_information_flow_ranking.*` — Conversation Topic Ranking
- **What it shows**: Ranking of 27 molecular communication pathways by information flow in the Hippocampus, revealing major surges in amyloid and inflammatory crosstalk.

### 4. `Fig4d_nvu_pathway_circos_network.*` — The Amyloid Broadcast Network (Circos Wheel)
- **What it shows**: Circular chord diagram tracing *APP* (amyloid) signaling across hippocampal cell types in Control (left) vs Alzheimer's (right).

### 5. `Fig4e_ligand_receptor_communication_bubble.*` — The Exact Molecular Words Spoken
- **What it shows**: High-resolution bubble plot identifying specific ligand–receptor pairs (such as *APP–CD46* and *APP–SORL1*) driving hippocampal neurovascular disruption.
